Avalon Healing Center came to TymFlo with a clear concern: how do we build something real for our community without signing up for a monthly bill that climbs every time we need to add something?

That question shaped every decision in this project. The Resource Hub was built on tools your organization already has access to for free, designed so your own staff can manage it going forward — no tech team, no developer on call, no recurring fees.

This report summarizes exactly what was built, what was exceeded, what it costs to run, and where it can go from here.

What Was Built
✓
Public Resource Directory Anyone can visit and search — no account, no login, no barriers for the people who need help.
✓
Search, Filter & Sort Filter by support type, cost, and insurance acceptance. Results update instantly as you type.
✓
Staff-Only Add & Remove Password-protected area where your team adds new organizations or removes ones that are no longer active.

Restore Removed Records Removed organizations aren't deleted — staff can restore them any time without losing any data.

Link Back to Avalon "Back to Avalon" in the navigation and footer keeps visitors connected to your main website.

Smart Caching The site stores data locally for 60 minutes so it loads fast and stays under Airtable's free usage limits.
✓
Video Walkthrough A full animated walkthrough video showing the public view, staff login, adding, and removing resources.
✓
Written Staff Guide An illustrated, step-by-step guide with screenshots covering every task a staff member would need to do.
Where We Went Further
  • ⭐
    Staff guide is private — not public The written guide is only accessible after a staff member logs in. Clients browsing the public site will never see internal documentation.
  • ⭐
    Help is two clicks away, always The video walkthrough and written guide are both linked directly inside the staff dashboard. Staff never have to go looking for instructions.
  • ⭐
    Clear notice before submitting a record A plain-language heads-up appears at the top of the add form reminding staff that what they enter will be visible to the public — reducing mistakes before they happen.
  • ⭐
    Works on phones The entire site — public and staff — works cleanly on mobile devices. Staff can manage resources from a phone without anything breaking or overflowing.
The Budget Question

What this costs to run after today: $0 per month.

The Resource Hub is hosted on GitHub Pages — a free, permanent hosting service that requires no subscription and no maintenance contract. Your data lives in Airtable's free plan, which handles everything this site needs without ever hitting a paid tier.

There is no server to manage, no platform to renew, and no vendor who can raise your rates. You own the code. You own the data. If your needs grow, the path to expand is clear — and it starts from something that already works.
